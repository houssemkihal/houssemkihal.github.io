Houssem Kihal — Physicist & Founder of CelleX3D

I hold a master’s degree in Computational Physics and Modeling from Cergy-Paris University, where I worked on statistical physics and the modeling of active matter.

Today I’m the founder of CelleX3D, a 3D medical animation studio that helps biotech and healthcare companies turn complex science into clear, compelling visuals — from mechanism-of-action animations to patient education and investor storytelling.

The same instinct runs through both chapters: making the invisible visible. This site is a portfolio of the computational physics projects I worked on along the way, from active-matter simulations to quantum random walks.
